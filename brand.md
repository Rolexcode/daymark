# Brand — Daymark

_Status: established_

Daymark is a calm, warm, quietly disciplined daily planning tool. Its visual language should feel like a well-kept paper journal: intentional, human, and steady rather than gamified or loud.

## Palette

- Paper: `#f5f0e7`
- Deep paper: `#ebe3d6`
- Ink: `#1e2922`
- Muted ink: `#5f675f`
- Terracotta: `#c76645`
- Dark terracotta: `#9c432a`
- Sage: `#64836d`
- Soft sage: `#dfe8df`
- White paper: `#fffdf8`

Use terracotta for emphasis and sage for completion/success. Preserve warm neutrals throughout; avoid cool gray surfaces, neon rewards, and punitive red progress states.

## Typography

- Display: Georgia / Times-style serif
- Interface and body: Inter or a system sans-serif stack
- Numbers should be clear and tabular where comparisons matter.

## Voice

Calm, direct, and honest. Encourage consistency without shame. Prefer “Not yet” and “Return tomorrow” over failure language. Keep instructions short and specific.

## Interaction

Controls use generous touch targets, visible focus states, subtle motion, rounded cards, and restrained borders. All motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
